## Descripción (máx. 4000)

```
Lumi is a light spirit that lives with you all day. The less you scroll, the brighter she shines. And if you overdo it, she doesn’t scold you: she takes a nap.

YOUR ATTENTION IS LIGHT
Pick the apps that steal most of your time and a gentle limit for the day. Lumi doesn’t count minutes to throw them in your face: she just shines more or less depending on how things go. Radiant, happy, tired or dim.

SHE GOES ON ADVENTURES
While she has light left, Lumi sets off on an expedition. At night she comes back with a postcard, things for her collection and sometimes a creature friend. There are twenty places to discover, from the Moss Forest to the Lake of Moons.

A SHIELD THAT DOESN’T PUNISH
If you open a thief app after your limit, you’ll find Lumi taking a nap: “Are we really going in?” You can stop, or ask for five more minutes. Friction with love, never punishment.

NO GUILT
Lumi never dies or gets sick. Every day starts fresh. Streaks have rest days. And night mode lets her sleep, so you can rest too.

YOUR PRIVACY FIRST
Lumi uses Apple’s Screen Time and only learns when you’re getting close to your limit. She doesn’t see what you do inside your apps. What she knows about you lives on your iPhone. The account is optional, keeps her safe if you change iPhones, and you can delete it anytime from the app.

THE IMPORTANT STUFF IS FREE
Your Lumi and her shield, the daily limit, expeditions and postcards, the widget and night mode are free. Always.

LUMI PLUS (OPTIONAL)
For those who want to go a little further: more species and light colors, exclusive places and story chapters, multiple schedules and stats, and decor for her burrow. Yearly or monthly subscription with a 7-day free trial on the yearly plan. It renews automatically until you cancel it in Settings › your name › Subscriptions, at least 24 hours before renewal.

Privacy: https://<domain>/privacidad.html?lang=en
```
